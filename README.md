# 🍽️ CRAVEWISE — Your Intelligent Food Discovery Companion

> **Discover what you crave. Find where to eat it. Know what to order.**

CRAVEWISE is an interactive food discovery and restaurant recommendation platform designed to make choosing food easier, smarter, and more personalized.

Instead of simply showing restaurants, CRAVEWISE connects the entire food-discovery journey — from understanding a user's craving and preferences to recommending dishes, discovering nearby restaurants, exploring menus, comparing options, and getting directions.

---

## 🌟 What is CRAVEWISE?

Choosing what to eat can be surprisingly difficult. Users often have to search across multiple platforms to answer simple questions:

* What should I eat?
* Which cuisine matches my mood?
* Where can I find it nearby?
* Which restaurant is suitable for my budget or dietary preferences?
* What should I order from the menu?
* Which restaurant is the better match for me?

**CRAVEWISE brings these decisions together in one interactive experience.**

The platform combines personalized food recommendations with local restaurant discovery and smart menu exploration.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 🤖 1. Personalized Food Recommendation

Users can enter their food preferences such as:

* Cuisine
* Budget
* Mood
* Dietary preferences
* Food preferences

CRAVEWISE analyzes these preferences and presents a personalized food recommendation with a match score and explanation.

The recommendation includes:

* Recommended dish
* Match percentage
* Food description
* AI recommendation insight
* Compatibility breakdown
* Reasons behind the recommendation

Users can also try another recommendation or immediately search for restaurants serving the suggested dish.

---

## 📍 2. Restaurants Near You

CRAVEWISE includes a dedicated restaurant discovery experience that helps users find restaurants, cafés, and culinary spots around their location.

Users can:

* Use their current location
* Enter a location manually
* Search restaurants
* Filter restaurants
* Explore nearby dining options
* Discover restaurants matching their preferences

The interface is designed around the idea of moving from **"What do I want to eat?" → "Where can I eat it?"**

---

## 🔎 3. Smart Restaurant Filters

Restaurant discovery can be refined using multiple filters.

Available filtering concepts include:

* Cuisine
* Distance
* Dietary preferences
* Budget
* Restaurant search
* Sorting by AI match
* Rating
* Distance
* Budget
* Popularity

This allows users to quickly narrow down restaurants according to their needs.

---

## ✨ 4. AI Match & Best Match

CRAVEWISE highlights restaurants based on how well they match the user's preferences.

Restaurant cards can display:

* AI Match
* Rating
* Distance
* Restaurant category
* Highlighted dishes
* Description
* Reasons why the restaurant was selected

A dedicated **"Best Match For You"** experience helps users understand why a particular restaurant or food option fits their preferences.

---

## 📖 5. Smart Menu Exploration

Users can open restaurant details and explore menus through an interactive interface.

Menu features include:

* Menu search
* Category navigation
* Dish descriptions
* Prices
* Dietary/menu badges
* Recommended dishes
* "What's Good Here?" highlights
* AI-assisted ordering guidance

This turns a restaurant menu from a long list of items into a more guided discovery experience.

---

## ⚖️ 6. Restaurant Comparison

CRAVEWISE provides a comparison interface that allows users to evaluate restaurant options side by side.

Users can compare relevant restaurant information before deciding where to eat.

---

## ❤️ 7. Saved & Recently Explored Restaurants

Users can maintain a personal collection of restaurants they are interested in.

The platform supports:

* ❤️ Saved restaurants
* 🕐 Recently explored restaurants
